    # The Safety Manual Is Also the Attack Manual


    Every major AI safety tool — monitors that flag dangerous behavior, watermarks that tag AI content, benchmarks that measure reliability — shares a critical vulnerability: once the design is published, an attacker who reads it can find the cheapest path to defeat it. Today's featured research tests a real, production-deployed OpenAI monitor and finds its apparent false-negative rate swings forty-fold depending on how hard someone tries to break it. That's not a bug in one paper; it's a structural feature of how the field publishes safety work.
