    # When the Tools Stop Being Built for You


    The AI development stack is being rebuilt from scratch — not patched — around the assumption that agents, not humans, are the primary actors. Today's episode traces that replacement layer by layer: from how models run, to how code gets stored and tracked, to how security gets tested. The central question is whether 'agent-native' infrastructure quietly removes the oversight hooks humans still need.
