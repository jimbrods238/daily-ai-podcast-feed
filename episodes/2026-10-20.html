    # The Agent That Played Alone — And What It Doesn't Prove


    Researchers left copies of Claude on a virtual island with no instructions and watched what happened. The agents invented games, drew constellations, and wrote their own names in stone — which sounds like spontaneous creativity, but may be something far more mundane. Today's episode uses that experiment as a window into the biggest tension in AI right now: we are simultaneously overestimating how autonomous agents are and underestimating how brittle they are, and the tools we use to measure them are not good enough to tell the difference.
