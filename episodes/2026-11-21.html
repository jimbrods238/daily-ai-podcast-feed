    # The Exponential Curve Has a Blind Spot


    METR's updated time-horizon report — the most rigorous public measurement of what AI can actually do — reveals that AI progress is real and exponential, but only on tasks where right and wrong can be checked automatically. Judgment-heavy tasks are being left behind, and today's wave of product launches is quietly organized around that gap. The local AI and specialist model booms are happening exactly where the easy feedback signals are.
