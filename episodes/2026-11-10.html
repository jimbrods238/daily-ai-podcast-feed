    # Kill Switches Are Not the Same as Safety


    OpenAI published the engineering details of Control Room, a live system that can find and stop any AI model workload running across their research infrastructure. The story reveals something important: one of the world's leading AI labs has responded to fragile alignment research not by fixing alignment, but by building a supervision layer that doesn't trust it. Four independent academic papers today, covering alignment, compression, interpretability, and deception detection, all arrived at the same finding — every layer of the AI reliability stack has a blind spot in the layer below it.
