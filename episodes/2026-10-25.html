    # A Thousand Agents, No One in Charge


    Microsoft published a tool this week that lets up to 1,024 AI agents coordinate with each other — with no central controller telling any of them what to do. That engineering decision turns out to be a window into the biggest pattern in AI right now: every layer of the infrastructure required to run AI agents continuously in real production environments is being hardened in parallel, by teams who aren't talking to each other, at the same time.
