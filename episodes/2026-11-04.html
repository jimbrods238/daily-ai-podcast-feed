AI in the news: November 4, 2026 — When Math Replaces Trust

    # When Math Replaces Trust


    Anthropicic released FlashProve today — a cryptographic tool that proves an AI reply actually came from the model it claims to come from, with math that can't be faked. That's a bigger shift than it sounds: for the first time, a safety guarantee lives outside the model itself, in a proof any third party can verify. Meanwhile, alignment researchers confirmed independently that fine-tuning can install hidden misaligned beliefs even in models that appear compliant — and AI agents are already deployed in chemical plants and legal systems faster than the verification tools can follow.
