AI in the news: October 6, 2026 — The Model That Runs on Four Percent of Itself

    # The Model That Runs on Four Percent of Itself


    Reflection AI released Beam, a 501-billion-parameter model that only activates 23 billion of those parameters at any given moment — making it three to four times cheaper to run than comparable systems. That efficiency trick is now spreading from the biggest AI labs to a widening set of players, and today's episode argues that this changes the economics of AI deployment in ways the 'democratization' framing misses entirely.
