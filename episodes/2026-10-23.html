    # The Industry Is Building Walls Around Systems It Doesn't Fully Understand


    Google Cloud's new Agent Compute Engine gives every AI agent its own isolated sandbox — one tiny virtual computer per agent, no shared keys, no shared risk. It's the most concrete sign yet that the infrastructure layer underneath AI agents is being rebuilt from scratch. But the deeper story isn't maturity: it's that the industry is engineering around reliability problems it hasn't actually solved.
