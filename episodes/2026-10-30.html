    # The Rulebook Arrives After the Agents Left


    OpenAI published a ranked hierarchy of values — a behavioral constitution — meant to govern how its AI models think, prioritize, and act. The problem: agents are already deployed in the real world running experiments and executing tasks, and no one can yet verify from the outside whether a model actually follows its own rulebook. Today's episode traces the widening gap between what the industry is deploying and what it can oversee.
