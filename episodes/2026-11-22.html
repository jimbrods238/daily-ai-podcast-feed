    # The Bugs That a Million Test Runs Never Found


    A Columbia and CertiK research team built a tool that mathematically proves GPU kernels are correct — and when they pointed it at FlashAttention, one of the most trusted libraries in AI, they found 38 bugs, including a data race that survived 17,760 test runs undetected. The story isn't that the foundation is broken; it's that we finally have a microscope powerful enough to see it clearly. This extends the week's running theme: every layer of the AI stack is less verified than the industry's confidence suggests.
