    # When the IPO Filing Becomes the Safety Audit


    Anthropic's decision to go public forces the company to reconcile its years-long safety narrative with the hard disclosures required by securities law — and the gap between those two things is the clearest window yet into whether the AI industry's self-legitimizing safety stories hold up under external scrutiny. Today's episode traces how that tension connects to a broader pattern this week: that AI's oversight mechanisms are failing at every level simultaneously, from technical safety benchmarks to academic credibility to the stories companies tell investors.
