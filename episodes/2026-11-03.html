AI in the news: November 3, 2026 — The Hole in the Plumbing

    # The Hole in the Plumbing


    The agentic infrastructure this show spent the past week watching get built has a structural security vulnerability baked into the protocol that connects AI agents to external tools — and a new study measuring nearly ten thousand real attack scenarios found it succeeds nearly one in four times. More troubling: the vendors who were told about it before publication rejected the disclosure, and the benchmarks used to certify agent safety are themselves now under scrutiny for being miscalibrated. The field may not just have a safety gap; it may lack reliable instruments to know whether that gap is closing.
